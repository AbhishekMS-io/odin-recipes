Chicken Fried Rice

Description

Chicken fried rice is a quick and flavorful dish made with cooked rice, tender chicken, vegetables, and savory sauces, stir-fried together for a delicious and satisfying meal.

Ingredients

  • Cooked rice (preferably cooled)
  • Chicken (boneless, small pieces)
  • Oil
  • Garlic (minced)
  • Onion (chopped)
  • Mixed vegetables (carrot, beans, peas)
  • Egg (optional)
  • Soy sauce
  • Pepper powder
  • Salt
  • Spring onions (for garnish)

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a pan or wok and sauté garlic and onion until fragrant.
  2. Add chicken pieces and cook until fully done.
  3. Add vegetables and stir-fry on high heat for 2–3 minutes.
  4. Push everything to one side, scramble the egg (if using), then mix.
  5. Add cooked rice and toss well.
  6. Season with soy sauce, salt, and pepper; mix on high heat.
  7. Garnish with spring onions and serve hot.
