Chicken Biriyani

Description

Chicken biryani is a flavorful rice dish made with fragrant basmati rice, marinated chicken, aromatic spices, and herbs, slow-cooked together for rich taste and aroma.

Ingredients

  • Basmati rice
  • Chicken (medium pieces)
  • Onion (thinly sliced)
  • Tomato
  • Ginger-garlic paste
  • Yogurt (curd)
  • Green chilies
  • Biryani masala
  • Red chili powder
  • Turmeric powder
  • Garam masala
  • Fresh coriander leaves
  • Mint leaves
  • Lemon juice
  • Ghee or oil
  • Whole spices (bay leaf, cloves, cardamom, cinnamon)
  • Salt
  • Water

Steps

  1. Wash and soak basmati rice for 30 minutes, then partially cook and drain.
  2. Marinate chicken with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, spices, salt, lemon juice, mint, and coriander; rest for 30 minutes.
  3. Heat oil or ghee, fry onions until golden, and remove half for garnish.
  4. Add whole spices, tomatoes, and marinated chicken; cook until chicken is tender.
  5. Layer cooked rice over the chicken.
  6. Top with fried onions, mint, coriander, and a little ghee.
  7. Cover tightly and cook on low heat (dum) for 15–20 minutes.
  8. Gently mix and serve hot with raita or salad.
